# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Hiring managers, engineering leads and potential clients, mostly remote, who open the page for a minute or two to judge whether Sergio is worth contacting.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio of Sergio Deri, a Backend & Full Stack developer from Montevideo with a VoIP and contact center background. Success: a visitor sees the personal projects, trusts the career behind them, and sends a message.

## Brand Personality
Craftsman programmer: builds from scratch, understands every component. Quiet, exact, with a dry telephony sense of humour.

## Anti-references
Corporate templates. Loud cyberpunk: heavy glitch, flicker, chromatic aberration. Retro-web costume such as GeoCities pages or fake operating systems. Japanese characters used as decoration.

## Design Principles
- Effects stay almost imperceptible; the content leads.
- Personal projects get the most visual weight.
- Every fact comes from the resume or the owner; missing data is hidden, never invented.
- Content lives in data files and is easy to edit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic HTML, full support for prefers-reduced-motion.
