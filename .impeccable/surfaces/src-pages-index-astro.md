Scope: the single-page portfolio. Visitor mode: Experience, with a contact action.
Audience and job: hiring managers and clients decide whether to contact Sergio.
Proof: real resume history, four personal projects. Provisional CRT screens stand in for screenshots and are labeled as placeholders.
Constraints: brief-pinned fonts, 8px radius, 72/48/20 type scale, no Japanese characters, near-imperceptible effects, no flicker.
Memorable moment: the page reads as one SIP call, INVITE to BYE.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A night-shift terminal seen through a Tokyo side street: one craftsman's call log. Refuses the category default of a centred hero over equal icon cards and refuses loud cyberpunk.

OWN-WORLD: #050505 ground, fixed 120px hairline grid, violet glow from the header. Cyan for actions, magenta for the subtitle and hovers. Line-only asanoha and seigaiha horizon. Bordered cards with a DOS path set into the top edge. Duotone photograph with faint scanlines.

STORY: The visitor meets Sergio, reads a career from modem repair to platform engineering, finds four personal projects with the most weight, then sends a message from the header or the form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky header with name, anchors, icons and a cyan Contact me. Left: 72px name, magenta role and intro below, INVITE line last. Right: large duotone cutout of Sergio, about 460px wide, fading into the page. Below desktop width it sits centred above the text.

FORM: Pinned by the user through the grilling interview, candidate one of one. Direction pinned by the owner across the interview, so concept-seed was not run and there is no seed key. The SIP-call device implements the brief line asking for a subtle nod to telephony and SIP. Signature interaction: letter-by-letter name entrance ending on a solid block cursor; section labels advance through the SIP call.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
